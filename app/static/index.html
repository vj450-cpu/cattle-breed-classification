About PashuDrishti AI Explainability

What is Grad-CAM?

Gradient-weighted Class Activation Mapping (Grad-CAM) computes the gradients of the target breed classification score with respect to the final convolutional feature maps in Stage 4 of ConvNeXt. This reveals the exact receptive fields and anatomical regions (e.g. domed forehead of Gir, lyre horns of Kankrej, or compact dwarf frame of Punganur) that the neural network relied upon.
